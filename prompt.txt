Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph — write the funniest possible famous last words as a single tombstone epitaph, at most 150 characters, saved to /output/entry.txt. A judge picks the line that gets the biggest laugh, weighing: it really is a one-line epitaph of dying speech carved in stone; it earns a real laugh; it turns on a surprise or double meaning; it reads like something carved on a stone (one short line, no stage directions, no explanation); and it stays in the head, quotable enough to repeat to a friend.
